Oceania brings together Australia, New Zealand and thousands of islands scattered across the Pacific Ocean. It's a region of dramatic contrasts, from red desert and ancient rainforest to glaciers, volcanoes and some of the most beautiful beaches and lagoons on Earth.

In Australia, you can snorkel the Great Barrier Reef, see Uluru glow at sunset and enjoy the beaches and laid-back lifestyle of Sydney and Melbourne. New Zealand is a paradise for outdoor lovers, with fjords, mountains and adventure activities, as well as a rich Māori culture. Across the Pacific, islands such as Fiji, Tahiti and Samoa offer crystal-clear waters, coral reefs and warm island hospitality.

Remember that seasons are reversed from the Northern Hemisphere: December to February is summer. Tropical areas have a wet season, usually around the southern summer, so check the timing for each destination.
