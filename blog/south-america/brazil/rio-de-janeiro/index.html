Rio de Janeiro, Brazil

  1. Blog

  4. Rio de Janeiro

Few cities in the world have a setting like Rio de Janeiro, where green mountains plunge into the Atlantic and famous beaches line the shore. The "Cidade Maravilhosa" pairs world-class scenery with music, food and a joyful energy that's easy to fall in love with.

Ride the cable car up Sugarloaf Mountain, stand beneath Christ the Redeemer atop Corcovado, and spend lazy afternoons on Copacabana and Ipanema beaches. Wander the colorful Selarón Steps, explore the hilltop bohemian neighborhood of Santa Teresa, hike in Tijuca Forest, one of the world's largest urban forests, and catch a match or tour at the legendary Maracanã stadium.

Rio is warm year-round. Summer (December to March) is hot, humid and festive, with Carnival usually in February or early March. For comfortable sightseeing weather, April to June and August to October are great choices. Check our tours in Rio de Janeiro.
