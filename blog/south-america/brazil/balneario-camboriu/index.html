Balneário Camboriú

Santa Catarina, Brazil

  1. Blog
  2. South America
  3. Brazil
  4. Balneário Camboriú

Nicknamed the "Brazilian Dubai," Balneário Camboriú in Santa Catarina is where a dramatic skyline of high-rise towers meets a long, curving stretch of golden sand at Praia Central. It's a lively, walkable beach city with a buzzing promenade, great seafood and a nightlife scene that keeps going well past midnight in summer.

Don't miss the Unipraias cable car, which glides over Atlantic Forest hills between Praia Central and the calmer Laranjeiras Beach, and the Cristo Luz statue overlooking the bay. The scenic Interpraias road leads to quieter coves like Estaleiro and Estaleirinho, and the giant FG Big Wheel offers sweeping views of the coastline, especially at sunset.

Summer (December to March) is peak season, with warm water and big crowds, and New Year's Eve fireworks draw visitors from all over Brazil. For milder weather and fewer people, consider spring (September to November) or early autumn. Check our tours in Balneário Camboriú.

Travel tips

  • Getting there: The closest airport is Navegantes (NVT), about a 30 to 40 minute drive away. Florianópolis airport (FLN) is another option, roughly 1.5 hours by road.
  • Getting around: The center and Praia Central are easy to explore on foot, and local buses and ride-hailing apps cover the rest, including the beaches along the Interpraias road.
  • Food to try: Fresh seafood is the highlight, especially a "sequência de camarão," a multi-course shrimp feast typical of Santa Catarina.
  • Money tip: Cards are widely accepted, but carry a little cash for beach vendors and small kiosks.
  • Plan ahead: In summer and over New Year's, book accommodation early, as prices and demand rise sharply.
