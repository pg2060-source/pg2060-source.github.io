Perched about 2,600 meters up in the Andes, Bogotá is Colombia's vibrant capital, a city of world-class museums, creative neighborhoods and an ever-growing food scene. Its cool, spring-like climate and backdrop of green mountains give it a character all its own.

Start in La Candelaria, the historic center, with its colonial houses, street art and Plaza de Bolívar. The Gold Museum holds one of the world's largest collections of pre-Hispanic gold work, and the nearby Botero Museum is free to visit. Take the cable car or funicular up Monserrate for panoramic city views, browse the Sunday flea market in charming Usaquén, or join locals on Sunday's Ciclovía, when major roads close to cars. The Salt Cathedral of Zipaquirá makes an easy and unforgettable day trip.

Bogotá's weather is mild all year, but December to March and July to August tend to be drier. Bring layers: days can be sunny, while evenings get chilly.
