Cartagena is Colombia's most romantic city, a Caribbean port wrapped in centuries-old stone walls. Its historic walled city, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, is a maze of colorful balconies draped in bougainvillea, charming plazas and lively restaurants that come alive in the evening.

Stroll the Old Town and walk along the city walls at sunset, then explore the artsy Getsemaní neighborhood, known for its street art and buzzing nightlife. Visit the Castillo San Felipe de Barajas, an impressive Spanish fortress overlooking the city, and take a boat to the Rosario Islands for clear turquoise water and snorkeling. For a deeper look at the region's culture, consider a day trip to San Basilio de Palenque, known for its Afro-Colombian heritage.

Cartagena is hot year-round. December to April is the driest and most popular season, while the rest of the year brings more rain and humidity but fewer crowds.
