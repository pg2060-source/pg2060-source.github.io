South America is a continent of extremes and endless variety. Here you'll find the Andes, the longest continental mountain range in the world, the vast Amazon rainforest, Caribbean and Atlantic coastlines and some of the most spectacular waterfalls on the planet. It's also a place of lively cities, centuries of colonial history, Indigenous cultures and music that seems to play on every corner.

Our South America section starts with three countries we love: Brazil, Argentina and Colombia. Whether you're dreaming of skyscraper-lined beaches, the thunder of Iguazú Falls, colorful colonial streets or Carnival celebrations, you'll find inspiration and practical advice for planning your trip.
