Africa is a continent of breathtaking scale and diversity, home to more than fifty countries and countless languages, traditions and landscapes. Here you'll find golden savannas roamed by elephants, lions and giraffes, the vast Sahara Desert, tropical rainforests, snow-capped Mount Kilimanjaro and a long coastline of beaches and islands.

Travelers come to Africa for unforgettable safaris in places like the Serengeti and the Maasai Mara, to marvel at the pyramids of Giza and ancient Egyptian temples, to feel the spray of Victoria Falls, or to explore lively cities such as Marrakech and Cape Town. Food, music and art are just as rich and varied, and every region has its own flavors and rhythms.

The best time to visit depends a lot on where you go. In many safari areas, the dry season is ideal for spotting wildlife, while North Africa is most comfortable in spring and autumn.
