Europe packs an astonishing amount of history, culture and scenery into a relatively compact area. In just a few hours by train, you can travel between countries with different languages, cuisines and traditions, which makes it a wonderful place to explore more than one destination on a single trip.

Wander through Rome's ancient ruins, admire the art of Paris and Florence, explore the canals of Amsterdam and Venice or soak up the sun on Mediterranean beaches in Spain, Italy and Greece. Nature lovers can hike in the Alps, cruise through Norway's fjords or discover Scotland's misty highlands. Food is part of the adventure too, from tapas and pasta to pastries, cheese and wine.

Late spring and early autumn are lovely times to visit, with pleasant weather and fewer crowds than in summer. In December, many cities glow with festive Christmas markets.
