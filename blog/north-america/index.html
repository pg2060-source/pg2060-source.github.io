North America stretches from the Arctic tundra of Canada and Alaska to the tropical beaches of Mexico, Central America and the Caribbean. Along the way, you'll find mountains, deserts, forests, lakes and some of the most famous cities in the world.

Explore the energy of New York, the culture of Mexico City or the cosmopolitan charm of Montreal and Vancouver. Nature lovers can marvel at the Grand Canyon, Yellowstone, Banff and Niagara Falls, while the Caribbean islands and Mexico's coastlines are perfect for sun, snorkeling and relaxation. Central America adds rainforests, volcanoes and ancient Maya ruins to the mix.

With so much variety, there's a great destination in every season. Summer is ideal for the national parks of Canada and the United States, winter is perfect for skiing or escaping to the Caribbean, and spring and autumn are pleasant almost everywhere.
