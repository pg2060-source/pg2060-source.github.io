Asia is the world's largest continent, and it holds an incredible range of experiences, from the Himalayas and tropical islands to deserts, rice terraces and some of the most dynamic cities on the planet. It's home to ancient civilizations, major world religions and traditions that have shaped history for thousands of years.

You could walk along the Great Wall of China, watch the sunrise over Angkor Wat in Cambodia, admire the Taj Mahal in India, or wander between neon streets and peaceful temples in Japan. Southeast Asia is famous for its beaches and islands, while the Middle East offers ancient sites, souks and desert landscapes. And everywhere you go, the food is a highlight, from street-food stalls to elaborate feasts.

Because Asia is so vast, climates vary widely. Monsoon seasons, winters and summers fall at different times, so check the best season for each destination.
